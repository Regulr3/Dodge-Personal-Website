At Dell, I’ve led engineering teams deploying some of the largest AI and HPC data center infrastructure projects in the United States; notably xAI’s ‘Colossus’ AI supercluster and Bank of America’s data center infrastructure across North America.

I’ve founded and built several startups from the ground up where I lead product development/strategy/design, cloud infrastructure, go-to-market strategy/ client delivery, B2B sales, team/business operations, venture capital/fundraising, legal, and HR. I bring creativity, grit, and limitless energy to everything I do. In my free time, I enjoy spending time with my family, Formula 1, sim racing, tennis, football, and tinkering with the newest tech gadgets. I'm a proud husband and father of two.

How I deliver a GPU cluster
Comet Cloud

At Comet Cloud I lead HGX B300 builds end to end. Every cluster goes through the same five stages; scroll to walk through them.

01

Rack & stack

Racks land, get positioned and dressed. HGX B300 nodes, leaf switches and storage go in and are cabled to the elevation plan.

Rack elevationsCable plansAsset tracking
02

Power & liquid cooling

Feeds and PDUs are energized and verified. Liquid cooling loops are filled, pressure tested and balanced before a single GPU powers on.

PDUsCDUsLeak & pressure tests
03

Fabric

The InfiniBand compute fabric and Spectrum-X Ethernet come up across leaf and spine, and every link is checked against the design.

InfiniBandSpectrum-XUFM / NetQ
04

Bring-up & validation

Firmware is baselined, nodes are imaged and the cluster is burned in. NCCL tests prove bandwidth across every GPU before a customer sees it.

Firmware baselineBurn-inNCCL tests
05

Customer acceptance

Acceptance runs against the agreed performance targets, then the cluster is handed over with monitoring and documentation in place.

Acceptance testsObservabilityHandover
